Shard of glass  
the open wound  
caught in my eye  
(but invisible)  

Point of knife  
snail slithers  
snakes slip between  
my fingertips  

Dead of night  
a blacker sun  
will never  
rise  

The past is  
what we wear  
inside  

What feels  
like life  
(but inexpressible)  

Look down below  
I cannot see  
my broken arm  
my bloody knee  

Ray of light  
reverse the flow  
of memories  

Return to self  
a different me  
imperceptible velocity
