Dr. Steffen Wöll

Research Statement

Researching how literature and culture imagine, map, and transform space.

About

I am a postdoctoral researcher in American Studies at Leipzig University. My work explores cartography, mobility, borders, and imperial imaginaries across American literature and culture. My research interests also extend to digital humanities, visual culture, film, horror, and game studies.

Current Project

A Place Between Oceans: Imagining American Empire, 1880–1940

The project examines how transoceanic and archipelagic imaginaries shaped U.S. imperial expansion and literary culture at the turn of the twentieth century.

Research Areas

Spatial Imaginaries

Historical discourses of space, critical cartography, and the cultural production of place.

Oceans and Empire

Archipelagic and island studies, mobility, borders, U.S. empire, and globalization.

Popular and Digital Culture

Intersections of popular and digital culture, with particular interests in horror and game studies.
